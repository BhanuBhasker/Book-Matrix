Import events from CSV

Required columns: title, description, category, venue, city, starts_at, ends_at, price, plus capacity for general admission. Optional: address, image_url, featured, seating (GENERAL or RESERVED) with seat_rows, seats_per_row. Dates look like 2027-01-15T19:30. Every row is validated first; invalid or duplicate rows are skipped and listed below. A ready-made sample is in the project at data/sample-events.csv.
